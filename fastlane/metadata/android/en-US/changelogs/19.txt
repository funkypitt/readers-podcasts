1.2.0 — Seasons. A channel cut into seasons shows them as headings that open and fold at a touch. A serial is read from season 1 down, first episode first, and is kept whole instead of being cut to its last fifty episodes (refresh the channel once to get the earlier seasons back). Importing subscriptions reads four feeds at a time and says how many could not be read. The exported subscriptions file is always one other programs can read.
